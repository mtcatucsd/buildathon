Welcome

Thank you for coming to our first buildathon! We are super excited to have you all here and, inshaAllah, build something really amazing for the community. Before you all get started, we wanted to go over some information about the event and the organizations that made this possible.

Our sponsors

Our sponsors for today's event are Manara West and Replit.

Replit

Replit is a software company founded in 2016 by Amjad Masad. Its AI Agent turns plain language into working software, so you can go from idea to a live app in minutes.

Manara West

Manara West, formerly MSA West, is a California nonprofit that supports Muslim students across West Coast campuses. It offers resources, community and mentorship, and hosts an annual conference where students from different universities meet. Manara is the partner behind our competition tracks for today!

About MTC

Now let's move on to a brief introduction of MTC.

Alhamdulillah, I have been privileged with serving as this year's president of MTC, also known as the Muslim Tech Collaborative at UCSD. We host social events with other Muslim student organizations, and we've hosted workshops teaching students how to build websites, 3D print, and build resumes and portfolios. Whether you are in tech or have an interest in it, our organization is for you. Our focus as an organization is primarily to help build up students emerging into their respective fields and to serve the community intentionally.

If this is something that interests you, please scan the QR code to our Linktree to get our socials, club website, and more information on how to get involved and stay up to date on our upcoming events.
