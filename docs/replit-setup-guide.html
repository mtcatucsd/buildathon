Before you check out

A card is required.
Here is how to avoid surprise charges.

30

days of Replit Core once you redeem the code.
After that it is a subscription and renews unless you cancel.

Card

Credit or debit card

You need a valid card to activate Core, even with the promo code applied.

Cancel

Cancel before renewal

Core is a subscription. If you do not want to continue after 30 days, cancel before it renews. Set a reminder on day 25.

Limits

Set a spending limit

In your account's usage and billing settings, set a spending limit so usage cannot run past what you expect.

Usage

Turn off Auto Reload

Auto Reload tops up your balance automatically. Turn it off, and check your usage regularly while you build.

Important

Replit is not responsible for additional usage charges. You are in control of your plan, your limits, and your cancellation.
